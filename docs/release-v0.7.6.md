## Power Audio is now MacAmp

Look for **MacAmp.app** in Finder and **Open MacAmp** under the three-bar menu bar icon. The app now uses English throughout, including effect controls, preset collections, accessibility labels, the audio permission prompt, and troubleshooting messages. The README, screenshots, and release notes are also in English.

### Upgrade from Power Audio

1. Quit Power Audio using the three-bar menu.
2. Download [MacAmp v0.7.6 for Apple Silicon](https://github.com/Fikriazrbni/MacAmp/releases/download/v0.7.6/MacAmp-v0.7.6-macos-arm64.zip), unzip it, and move **MacAmp.app** to **Applications**.
3. Open MacAmp. The new app reads your existing preset and intensity settings. Click the **three-bar icon → Open MacAmp** to open the main window.

Requires macOS 14.2 or later. This build is ad hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. To verify the download, save **SHA256SUMS.txt** next to the ZIP and run `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt` in that folder.
